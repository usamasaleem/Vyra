# Knowledge Hub Structure

## Purpose

The Knowledge Hub is the single source of truth for product, operations, technical knowledge, decisions and reference material.

Goals:

- Find answers quickly
- Avoid duplicate information
- Keep current decisions visible
- Separate current facts from historical information

---

Rules:

- Do not delete important history
- Move replaced documents here

Date

---

## Summary

The key information someone needs quickly.

## Details

Full explanation.

## Decisions

Important choices and reasoning.

## Related Pages

Links to connected information.

---

# Knowledge Hub Rules

1. Keep information in one place.
2. Prefer short clear documents.
3. Update source documents instead of duplicating.
4. Archive old information instead of deleting it.
5. Give important documents an owner.
6. Separate current facts, proposed ideas and historical context.

---

# Success Criteria

The Knowledge Hub works when:

- New team members understand the project quickly.
- People can find answers without asking repeatedly.
- Decisions have clear reasoning.
- Everyone works from the same information.
