# Vyra — Project Context

## Vision
Vyra is building an AI-powered assistant for luxury and exotic car rental businesses, starting with WhatsApp-based customer enquiries.

The goal is to help rental operators handle enquiries reliably while keeping sales teams in control of commercial decisions.

## Target Market
Initial focus:
- Dubai luxury and exotic car rental operators.
- Teams handling multiple customer enquiries.

## Customer Problem
Customers need fast answers about:
- Vehicle availability
- Rental dates
- Pricing
- Deposits
- Delivery
- Requirements

Businesses need:
- Faster responses
- Consistent information
- Better enquiry tracking
- Clear ownership

## Product Direction
Initial workflow:
Customer enquiry → AI qualification → human sales handoff → confirmed booking.

Future areas:
- Inventory management
- Owner tools
- Sales insights
- Rental lifecycle integrations

## Principles
- Reliability first
- Human control over commitments
- Approved data over assumptions
- Clear ownership
- Honest communication
