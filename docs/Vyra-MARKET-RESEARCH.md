# Vyra — Market Research

## Purpose

This document contains only market-related information for Vyra's car rental AI assistant business. Product design, technical implementation, and engineering notes are intentionally excluded.

---

# 1. Target Market

## Primary Market

**Dubai luxury and exotic car rental businesses**

Target operators:
- Luxury car rental companies
- Exotic/supercar rental fleets
- Premium chauffeur and vehicle experience providers
- Businesses handling high-value rental enquiries through WhatsApp

## Market Characteristics

- High-value transactions
- Customers compare multiple operators before booking
- Fast response speed can influence purchase decisions
- Availability, pricing, deposits, insurance, and delivery details are key buying factors
- Many enquiries require qualification before a salesperson can close the booking

---

# 2. Customer Demand

## Customer Enquiry Patterns

Common customer questions:
- Vehicle availability
- Rental pricing
- Deposit requirements
- Insurance information
- Delivery options
- Rental duration
- Eligibility requirements

Customer expectations:
- Quick answers
- Accurate information
- Smooth WhatsApp communication
- Less repetition during booking discussions

---

# 3. Business Problems in the Market

Luxury rental operators may face:

- Slow responses during busy periods
- Lost enquiries due to delayed follow-up
- Sales teams spending time on repetitive questions
- Difficulty tracking customer interest
- Limited visibility into enquiry-to-booking performance

---

# 4. Market Opportunity

Vyra's market opportunity is based on helping rental operators improve:

- Enquiry handling
- Customer qualification
- Sales visibility
- Follow-up consistency
- Booking pipeline management

The value proposition is operational improvement rather than guaranteed revenue growth.

---

# 5. Target Customer Profile

## Ideal Early Customers

Characteristics:
- Dubai-based luxury/exotic rental operators
- Small to medium sales teams
- Multiple daily WhatsApp enquiries
- Valuable vehicle inventory
- Need better enquiry organization

Initial hypothesis:
- Operators with approximately 20–150 vehicles may be suitable prospects.

This vehicle range is a prospecting assumption and requires validation.

# 7. Market Validation Questions

Key questions:

1. How many luxury rental operators in Dubai match the target profile?
2. How many enquiries does an average operator receive daily?
3. What percentage of enquiries become bookings?
4. How much revenue is lost from slow responses?
5. What tools are operators currently using?
6. How much would operators pay for improved enquiry management?
7. Which workflows create the most operational pain?
