07 Open questions

What's real, and what's still open

Business problems in the market
What luxury operators report struggling with today
  • Slow responses during busy periods
  • Lost enquiries from delayed follow-up
  • Sales time lost to repetitive questions
  • Difficulty tracking customer interest
  • Little visibility into enquiry-to-booking performance
Market validation questions
