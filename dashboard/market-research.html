What's real, and what's still open

07

The demand-side problems Vyra is meant to fix, and the questions that still need a real operator's answers before any of the above becomes a business plan.

Business problems in the market

What luxury operators report struggling with today
  • Slow responses during busy periods
  • Lost enquiries from delayed follow-up
  • Sales time lost to repetitive questions
  • Difficulty tracking customer interest
  • Little visibility into enquiry → booking performance

Market validation questions

Unanswered before any number above can be trusted
  1. How many luxury operators in Dubai actually match the target profile?
  2. How many enquiries does an average operator receive daily?
  3. What share of enquiries become bookings?
  4. How much revenue is lost to slow responses?
  5. What tools are operators using today?
  6. What would operators actually pay for this?
  7. Which workflows create the most operational pain?
