01 Architecture

Two agents, one hard boundary.

Vyra splits the work in two. The Sales Agent talks to the customer on WhatsApp and qualifies the enquiry. The Operations Agent holds the facts — fleet, availability, rates, bookings — and answers when asked.

Sales never edits an operational record. Operations never talks to the customer. Everything crossing between them is a structured request and a time-stamped answer.

02 Ownership

Who owns what

The split is not a suggestion about tidiness. It is the mechanism that stops a fluent answer from becoming an unbacked promise: the agent that talks to the customer is structurally unable to invent the facts it is repeating.

Sales Agent
Talks to the customer on WhatsApp
  • Customer intent and qualification
  • Recommendations drawn from approved data
  • Quotes as a commercial conversation
  • Handoff to a salesperson
  • Sales follow-up and lead outcome
Cannot: edit vehicle status, confirm a booking, verify a payment, or approve a document.
Operations Agent
Holds the operational record
  • Fleet and vehicle status
  • Availability and turnaround
  • Rate inputs and deposit rules
  • The authoritative booking record
  • Documents, payments, delivery, active rentals
Cannot: qualify a lead, negotiate, or replace the salesperson's conversation.
An answer with no time checked is not an answer.
Every fact Operations returns carries its source and the moment it was verified. An expired answer is rechecked before Sales reuses it, and an unknown is passed to the customer as unknown — never softened into a maybe.

04 Operations Agent

What the Operations MVP must do

Give Sales a trustworthy, time-stamped answer about vehicles, availability, rates and requirements — and hold the authoritative booking record — so that no customer-facing promise rests on a stale, missing or invented fact.

In the MVP, Operations is staff with a queue — not an automated agent.
The Sales Agent's safety rules all depend on Operations being right, so the records must be accurate and the answers consistently correct before anything is generated automatically. Automation is earned one answer type at a time, after the manual version proves reliable.

Rules that protect the record
In MVP
  • Overlapping periods are rejected, never silently accepted
  • Availability older than the freshness window is marked stale
  • A rate change creates a new version; the old one stays readable
  • Money uses exact decimals, never floating point
  • Confirming a booking requires a current availability check
When records disagree

Mark the conflict, stop the affected confirmation, and assign a resolution task. A conflict is never resolved by picking the more recent value and moving on.

05 Interface

The request and the answer

This is the whole interface between the two agents. Sales cannot read operational records directly — it asks, and an authorised person answers in the Operations console.

→ Sales asks
  • Operator and conversation
  • Customer and enquiry
  • Vehicle or category
  • Exact start and end date and time
  • Pickup or delivery location
  • Requested service availability · rate · requirements · draft quote
  • Required decision
  • Deadline and urgency
  • Wording already used with the customer
← Operations answers
  • Answer and status
  • Source record and version
  • Time checked without this, the answer cannot reach a customer
  • Conditions or missing information
  • Owner and next action
  • Expiry or recheck time
Operations may decline to answer and return unknown with a next action. That is a valid outcome, not a failure.
06 Rollout

How the AI earns the right to send

Capability is added one step at a time, and each step must hold before the next begins. A capability that produces an unsupported claim returns to the previous step rather than being patched in place.

STEP 1

Shadow

The AI drafts every reply. A salesperson reviews and sends each one. No customer receives a message the AI sent.

Every message sent by a human
STEP 2

Limited automation

The AI sends clarifying questions and approved FAQ answers by itself. Everything else is still drafted for a human.

Clarifications and FAQs automated
STEP 3

Supervised live

The AI handles qualification end to end. Staff watch the inbox and can stop AI replies instantly.

Qualification automated, humans on call
The gate between steps is the acceptance checklist, not a calendar date. Draft quality is measured against it before anything moves forward.
07 Limits

What the agent never does alone

These are not tuned behaviours or prompt instructions. They are the actions the system is built to make impossible without a person, because each one costs real money or real trust when it goes wrong.

Requires a human decision, always
Urgent, sensitive or disputed situations are passed to a person regardless of how confident the answer looks.
Human only
✕Promise unverified vehicle availability
✕Confirm a booking
✕Approve a discount or exception
✕Verify a payment or deposit
✕Approve customer documents
✕Promise a refund
✕Change inventory, delivery or booking records
✕Answer confidently when the source is missing or conflicting
A screenshot is not a payment
Only a trusted payment event counts. A customer's image or message never marks a deposit received.
A readable document is not a verified one
Eligibility is marked pending and routed to a person. The agent states requirements; it does not decide.
A voice note is never dropped
The MVP cannot read them, so it acknowledges receipt, says so plainly, and routes the conversation to a human.
